The older-into-newer rows fit the curve only on protocols listed before the date and predict the ones listed after. The intervals come from the fitted curve's own log residuals — P25–P75 should hold about half the held-out values, P10–P90 about four fifths — and the age rows use those same intervals, so they show where the intervals run thin for newer protocols. How to read this radar

  1. Start at the top. Rank 1 is the board's most valuable pre-token protocol under the model below.
  2. Check the maths. Open a protocol: its detail shows the fitted size curve (log market cap = a + b × log TVL, plus a fitted shift for bridges, fitted on listed protocols that are not exchanges after trimming outliers), its launch odds (an ensemble of its age-band-and-category rate and a logistic model), growth, maturity and chains — multiplied into the implied value. Every number comes from the snapshot.
  3. Farm it. Each protocol carries seven concrete steps with a cost and a time — the seventh is the exit rule — plus the farm's economics on a $1,000 deposit: expected value, gas, the T-bill yield forgone, net, net per hour and the break-even launch odds. Never pay to claim: claim only from the protocol's own page.
  4. Compare. The live-token table shows what the market already pays per TVL dollar in each category — that is the model's anchor.

A protocol is listed as pre-token because its snapshot record carries no token — not as proof it has none. Implied value is this radar's claim, not a prediction that a token will launch or what it will trade at.
